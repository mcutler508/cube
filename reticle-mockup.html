Mockup · Cube reticle styles

Six directions for the direction indicator

Each tile shows one aesthetic take on the reticle that sits on every sticker. The large sample is the "hero" read; the three small chips below show how the same reticle reads across a warm face (red), a bright face (yellow), and a cool face (blue). All examples animate at rest — commit-flash brightening would layer on top of these.

Red face (warm) Yellow face (bright) Blue face (cool)
Option 01

Cyberpunk HUD crosshair

Corner brackets frame the piece like a target lock; short tick marks anchor each axis; a thin center cross keeps the read. Feels like a mech / shooter reticle — technical, decisive, "you are aiming."

Option 02

Holographic scan lines

Dashed axes at low opacity with a bright "scan segment" that sweeps along each bar on a loop. Feels lighter and floatier — a projected hologram rather than a painted marker. Naturally implies motion.

Option 03

Ink brush / gestural

Tapered brush strokes in warm off-white, painterly ends. Suggests martial-arts / rhythm-game / calligraphy energy — the piece is a canvas, the reticle is a mark. Less "UI overlay," more character.

Option 04

Arcade energy arrows

The bars terminate in chevron heads pointing outward on each axis — an explicit "spin here" cue. Chunky, high-contrast, fighting-game / DDR arrow language. Boldest of the six, hardest to ignore.

Option 05

Neon circuit

Thin traces from a central node out to four endpoint nodes. Endpoints have small square "pads." Nodes glow independently on a slow phase. Reads like a PCB or sci-fi rig terminal.

Option 06

Compass diamond

A slowly-breathing rhombus frame with pips at the four cardinal points and a minimal cross center. Least busy of the set — architectural, elegant. Reads as "compass rose" more than "targeting reticle."

These are 2D CSS/SVG mockups approximating how the reticle overlay would read on a live cube face. The actual implementation in Sticker.tsx lives in Three.js — thin emissive geometry floating just above each sticker top. Any of these can be built with a mix of thin mesh geometry, sprite planes, or SDF textures; complexity ranges from Option 06 (easy) to Option 04 / 05 (needs a few more small meshes per sticker).
